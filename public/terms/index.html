Terms for using Wrapline

Using Wrapline

You may use Wrapline to process audio you own or have permission to use. Check each output before publication and keep backups of source files and exported recipes.

Audio behavior

Wrapline offers browser-decodable WAV and MP3 inputs and exports 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Loudness is an RMS-based estimate with a ±12 dB gain cap. Peak control is sample-based, not a broadcast-certified EBU R128 or true-peak measurement. Browser codec support varies by operating system.

Studio purchase

The free tier saves one recipe and renders up to three tracks per batch. A $29 one-time Studio license unlocks unlimited saved recipes and batch size for the current major version. Checkout and refunds are handled by Sociobot / Dodo, the merchant of record. A refund, chargeback, expiry, or revocation makes a license inactive.

Availability

The software is provided “as is,” without warranties. Local processing can be limited by device memory, browser storage quotas, or unsupported or corrupt codecs.

Fair use

Do not attempt to bypass license verification, misuse the billing service, or use the software unlawfully. Accessibility, data export, and core safety disclosures remain available without payment.

Privacy

How local data and license checks work is described in the privacy policy.
