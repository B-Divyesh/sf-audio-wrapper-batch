Privacy, without fine-print fog.

Your audio does not leave your device. Wrapline decodes, mixes, previews, and packages files inside your browser.

What stays local

Recipe settings, optional intro/outro/bed files, production receipts, and your license token are stored locally using IndexedDB or localStorage. They stay until you delete site data, delete a recipe, or remove the app. You can export a recipe and its assets as JSON at any time.

License verification

If you restore or buy a Studio license, Wrapline sends the license token—not your audio—to the Sociobot billing API at most once per day. Sociobot and Dodo are the merchant of record and process checkout data under their own policies. Wrapline does not receive or store card details.

Analytics and network use

This version contains no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime CDN scripts. Network access is used only to load/update the app and verify a license. Once installed, free audio processing works offline.

Your choices

Delete an individual saved recipe in the app. Remove all local data using your browser’s site-data controls. Export recipes and receipts before clearing data if you want a copy.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through Sociobot. This policy will be updated if Wrapline’s data behavior changes.
