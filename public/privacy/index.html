Privacy for Wrapline

Your audio does not leave your device during a render. Wrapline decodes, mixes, previews, and packages files inside your browser.

What stays local

Recipe settings, intro, outro, music bed files, and receipts use local browser storage. Export recipe JSON downloads the current recipe and its audio files.

Demo mode

The sample demo uses separate names beginning with demo:. Reset demo and Start for real delete that demo namespace. The demo does not open or copy real Wrapline recipes, receipts, or license state.

License verification

When you verify a Studio license, Wrapline sends the token—not your audio—to the product verification URL. A completed check for the same token is reused for one day.

Analytics and network use

This version has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime CDN scripts. A render sends no network requests outside Wrapline. The installed demo can render offline after its first visit.

Your choices

Delete an individual saved recipe in the app. Remove all local data using your browser’s site-data controls. Export recipes and receipts before clearing data if you want a copy.
