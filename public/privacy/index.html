Privacy for Wrapline

Your audio does not leave your device during a render. Wrapline decodes, mixes, previews, and packages files inside your browser.

What stays local

Recipe settings, optional intro/outro/bed files, production receipts, and your license token are stored locally using IndexedDB or localStorage. You can export a recipe and its assets as JSON at any time.

Demo mode

The sample demo uses separate names beginning with demo:. Reset demo and Start for real delete that demo namespace. The demo does not open or copy real Wrapline recipes, receipts, or license state.

License verification

If you restore or buy a Studio license, Wrapline sends the license token—not your audio—to the Sociobot billing API at most once per day. Sociobot and Dodo are the merchant of record and process checkout data under their own policies. Wrapline does not receive or store card details.

Analytics and network use

This version has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime CDN scripts. Network access loads or updates the app and verifies a license. The installed demo can render offline after its first visit.

Your choices

Delete an individual saved recipe in the app. Remove all local data using your browser’s site-data controls. Export recipes and receipts before clearing data if you want a copy.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through Sociobot (external site). This policy will be updated if Wrapline’s data behavior changes.
